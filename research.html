Research

What I work on

Broadly, I'm interested in the geometric structure of classical and kinetic physical theories, and in building numerical methods that preserve that structure rather than approximate it away. The three groupings below are how I think about it — not a flat list.

01

Mathematical foundations of classical dynamics

Classical mechanics, done properly, is geometry: phase space carries a symplectic structure, and the equations of motion are the flow generated by a Hamiltonian function on that structure. I'm interested in this picture at both ends — the general Hamiltonian and Lagrangian formalism, and concrete gravitating systems like the N-body problem, where the same structure has to survive contact with real, messy dynamics (resonances, near-integrability, chaos).

\[ \dot q = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p}, \qquad \dot p = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q} \]

02

Structure-preserving computation

A generic numerical integrator gets the trajectory approximately right and the invariants wrong — energy drifts, angular momentum leaks, the symplectic form is violated. Structure-preserving methods instead design the discretization around the invariant: symplectic and geometric integrators, operator-splitting schemes that decompose a hard flow into simpler flows that are individually exact or structure-respecting, and the numerical analysis that explains why this buys long-time qualitative correctness instead of just short-time accuracy.

\[ \varphi_t^{*}\,\omega = \omega, \qquad \omega = \sum_i dq_i \wedge dp_i \]

a symplectic integrator's flow map preserves the canonical 2-form exactly

03

Physical systems that stretch the geometric picture

The clean Hamiltonian picture has to be extended to be physically useful: to the kinetic description of a plasma, where phase-space structure meets statistical evolution; to systems with genuine dissipation, where a metriplectic (GENERIC) bracket combines reversible Hamiltonian flow with an irreversible, entropy-producing part; and to general relativity, where the phase space itself is dynamical and gravitational waves are its clearest observable signature.

\[ \dot z = \{z, H\} + (z, S) \]

metriplectic (GENERIC) evolution: a Poisson bracket for reversible dynamics plus a symmetric, dissipative bracket driving entropy production
