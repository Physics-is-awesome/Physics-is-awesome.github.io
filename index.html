Physics · Mathematical Physics · Computational Physics

I'm an undergraduate physicist interested in the mathematical structure underneath physical theories, and in numerical methods that respect it. My current work sits between Hamiltonian and geometric mechanics, structure-preserving numerical methods (symplectic and geometric integration, operator splitting, metriplectic dynamics), and applications in plasma physics, general relativity, and celestial mechanics. I write up derivations and half-finished ideas in my notes, build small research-grade tools in my projects, and occasionally turn a piece of physics into a video on YouTube.
